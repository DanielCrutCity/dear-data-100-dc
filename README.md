# dear-data-100-dc
My Dear Data 100 project:

This is a personal data collection and creative coding project, exploring my everyday commute, between Northwood Underground station as well as Farringdon station - my regular commute to university, as well as other locations.

The project all of the events that shape  the experience of travelling through London: I have created a data-logging workflow, using Apple's shortcuts app on my iPhone 17. The purpose of this system is to make recording observations during my commutes, quick and consistent, without having to manually write down every field for each entry - it also provides location information through coordinates, which I can use to map where I was along the Northwood --> Farringdon journey, as well as any other commutes I may have taken during the time period of my research. 

The shortcut follows a sequence of actions, with each action contributing a different piece of information to the final record, this is description of the sequence of actions: 
1. Captures the current location, both latitude and longitude.
2. Records the date & time, allowing me to distinguish when records were taken etc.
3. Event category selection:

   The shortcut gives me the ability to choose between which event has occurred during my commute, here are some of the events:

- Waiting or delays
- Crowding or congestion
- Navigation or signage issue
- Obstruction or accessibility issue
- Disruptive behaviour
- Environmental condition

4. Impact rating - selecting how impactful or disturbing the event was to my commute.
   Choose between Minor, Noticeable, Major.

5. Duration of the event, to gain information on the delay as well as supporting its severity.

6. The shortcut then combines the information and then is sent to a notes app onto my phone, in a clean format for me to publish into my "commute_observations.csv" file.

During the later stage of my project, involving the processing sketch - I aim to have all of the data displayed along the tube map of the journeys I have taken, and how far along these events have happened - every single event will be represented as shapes / symbols.
